04 FAQ

Common questions

Which social media platforms do you manage?

Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat, LinkedIn, X and Facebook. We recommend platforms based on where your customers spend their time: for consumer brands in Oman and the GCC that is usually Instagram, Snapchat and TikTok, and for B2B it is LinkedIn.

Do you create the content as well?

Yes. Our team handles design, short video, photography and copywriting in Arabic and English, so one team covers planning, production and posting.

How do you measure social media results?

We track reach and engagement, but we report on what matters to the business: enquiries, leads, website visits and sales from social, with tracking set up from the start.
