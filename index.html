FAQ

Questions, answered.

How fast can an AI agent go live for my business?

Starter deployments — chat agents, FAQ automation and social media response — typically go live within one to two weeks. Full growth systems with CRM automation and voice agents are deployed in phases across four to eight weeks.

Do your AI agents work in Arabic?

Yes. Our agents handle Arabic and English natively, including Gulf dialects — which matters when you are serving customers across Oman, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Kuwait, Qatar and Bahrain.

Will AI replace my team?

No. It removes the repetitive work that slows your team down. AI handles instant replies, data entry and follow-ups, while your people focus on strategy, relationships and closing deals.

We already work with a marketing agency. Can you still help?

Yes. We can operate as the AI and automation layer alongside your existing agency, or take on the full stack. Most clients consolidate once they see what one connected system does to their numbers.

How do you measure results?

Every engagement is tied to clear KPIs — response time, lead capture rate, conversion rate and revenue influenced — reported in a transparent monthly dashboard. No vanity metrics.

Do you only work with businesses in Oman?

We are based in Muscat and work across the GCC and wider MENA region — including Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Kuwait, Qatar and Bahrain. Most of our delivery is remote, with on-site work in Oman when a project needs it.
