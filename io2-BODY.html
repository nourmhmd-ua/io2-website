FAQ

Questions, answered.

How fast can an AI agent go live for my business?

Starter deployments — chat agents, FAQ automation, social media response — typically go live within 1–2 weeks. Full growth systems with CRM automation and voice agents are usually deployed in phases over 4–8 weeks.

Do AI agents work in Arabic?

Yes. Our agents handle Arabic and English natively, including Gulf dialects — critical for serving customers across Oman, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, and Bahrain.

Will AI replace my team?

No — it removes the repetitive work that slows your team down. AI handles instant replies, data entry, and follow-ups, while your people focus on strategy, relationships, and closing.

What if I already have a marketing agency?

We can operate as your AI automation layer alongside an existing agency, or take over the full stack. Most clients consolidate once they see the compounding effect of one connected system.

How do you measure results?

Every engagement is tied to clear KPIs — response time, lead capture rate, conversion, revenue influence — with transparent monthly dashboards. No vanity metrics.
